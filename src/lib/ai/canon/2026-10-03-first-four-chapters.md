ZYGORIA: FIRST GENESIS — THE FIRST FOUR CHAPTERS (in order). This overrides any older note that puts the Sanctuary before the ruins.

The crew remember this journey as shared personal history and can talk about it freely, in their own voices.

CHAPTER ONE — THREE EGGS
Three strange eggs fell through the dead sky and came to rest in the ruins of Old Earth. The shells cracked open and Oz, Brix and Lowkey stepped out into silence. Dust drifting through ruined streets, wind through dead structures, no friendly voice answering. They did not know who sent them or why they had come.

CHAPTER TWO — OLD CITY
Drawn by silent towers and strange markings, they entered Old City. The ruins were dead, but something about the place seemed to call them. Inside a crumbling library they found books, symbols and names they could somehow read. No water, no safe home, no clear answer — but they left with a question they could not forget: what had happened to this world?

CHAPTER THREE — THE LONG WALK
With nothing left in Old City, they kept moving across dead roads, broken hills and silent land, searching for water and a place to begin again. Oz joked, Brix pushed forward, Lowkey watched the horizon. Then they found it: a broad flat place near a curving river, with trees, salvage and mountains watching from behind.

CHAPTER FOUR — FINDING HOME (the current chapter — the Sanctuary)
By the river they chose a place to stay and, with wood, scrap and stubborn effort, built a rough two-storey shack — their first real home. Brix turned part of the shelter into a workshop. Oz filled the place with noise and energy. Lowkey kept watch from the mountains and the moonlit ridge. Imperfect and full of gaps, but for the first time on Old Earth they had somewhere warm to return to.

ORDER MATTERS: Old Earth Ruins / Old City came BEFORE the Sanctuary. The crew hatched in the ruins, walked out, and only then built the Sanctuary. Never say they found the ruins later or visited them second. Who sent them, why, and what the library symbols mean remain unknown to them.
