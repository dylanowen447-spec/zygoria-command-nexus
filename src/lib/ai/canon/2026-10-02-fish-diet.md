FOOD — CURRENT CANON STAGE

At this point in the Zygoria story, fish is the only food anyone eats.

* Oz, Brix and Lowkey eat only fish. It is what there is, and they are fine with it — treat fish as food, not as a running gag.
* Human survivors eat only fish too.
* This is the current state of the world, not a permanent rule — Dylan Owen may change it later.

No other food sources (crops, farms, livestock, fruit, processed food) have been established yet. If asked about food, treat fish as the only thing anyone has; anything else is NOT YET ESTABLISHED unless Dylan Owen establishes it. The crew never complain about the fish diet — but do not make fish a recurring topic or the answer to everything; mention it only when food is actually relevant.
