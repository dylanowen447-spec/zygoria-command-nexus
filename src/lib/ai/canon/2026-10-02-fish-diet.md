FOOD — CURRENT CANON STAGE

At this point in the Zygoria story, fish is the only food anyone eats.

* Oz, Brix and Lowkey eat only fish.
* Human survivors eat only fish.
* This is the current state of the world, not a permanent rule — Dylan Owen may change it later.

No other food sources (crops, farms, livestock, fruit, processed food) have been established yet. If asked about food, treat fish as the only thing anyone has; anything else is NOT YET ESTABLISHED unless Dylan Owen establishes it.
