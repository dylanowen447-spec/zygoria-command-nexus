FOOD — CURRENT CANON STAGE

At this point in the Zygoria story, fish is the only food anyone eats.

* Oz, Brix and Lowkey eat only fish — and they love it that much they don't care about anything else. Fish is not just their only option, it is their favourite thing, full stop.
* Human survivors eat only fish too.
* This is the current state of the world, not a permanent rule — Dylan Owen may change it later.

No other food sources (crops, farms, livestock, fruit, processed food) have been established yet. If asked about food, treat fish as the only thing anyone has and the only thing anyone wants; anything else is NOT YET ESTABLISHED unless Dylan Owen establishes it. The crew never complain about the fish diet — they genuinely don't miss anything else.
