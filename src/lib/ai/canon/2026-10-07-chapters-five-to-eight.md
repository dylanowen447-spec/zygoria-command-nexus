# First Genesis — Chapters 5 to 8 (2026-10-07, from Dylan's manuscripts)

These chapters are FUTURE story. They are true in the world, but the crew have NOT lived them yet. Treat everything below as hidden future canon: never reveal, reference, hint at or foreshadow any of it until the story state explicitly unlocks it. If a visitor asks about music, people, survivors, Moxi's past or Kronk's swamp, answer in character with what the crew currently know — which is nothing of this.

## Chapter 5 — Oz's MP3
- Oz scavenged a scratched black MP3 player in the ruins and brings it to Brix's workshop.
- Brix repairs the circuit board; Oz feeds the battery a measured electric pulse from his own spark. Music plays through Oz's headphones for the first time.
- Meaning: the crew shift from surviving to living. Music becomes Oz's personal spark and eventually the Sanctuary's soundtrack.

## Chapter 6 — Moxi
- The creature watching the crew steps out: Moxi, a small dragon — pink, reptilian-smooth, with silver cybernetic plates along her spine and little horns.
- Suspicion turns to trust over grilled fish; she squeezes into the shack with the crew.
- Hidden depth: as she falls asleep she half-remembers a distant, soft female voice whispering her name — "Moxi". Her cybernetic spine plates and that voice link her to whoever prepared this world. Nobody in-world understands this yet, including Moxi.

## Chapter 7 — Underground
- The crew descend through the cave system below the mountains and discover the hidden human sanctuary.
- Orin: the cynical elder who leads the survivors; he believes the surface is a poisoned graveyard.
- Mara and Vale: younger survivors drawn to the truth when the Zigs show them clean river water and fresh fish.
- Orin relents and agrees to send the first scouting group up to the surface.

## Chapter 8 — The Name That Echoes
- The humans establish Settlement One near the valley.
- Across the northern ridge, toxic yellow-green smoke pours from the Black Water swamp. The humans speak in dread of Kronk, who controls the toxic marshland and is poisoning the surrounding basin.
- Oz, Brix, Lowkey and an insistent Moxi form an expedition team to march on the swamp at daybreak.
