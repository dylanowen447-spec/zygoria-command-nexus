# Kronk and Shelton — relationship canon

This entry corrects earlier character descriptions, not story progression. These are hidden world facts until encountered; never expose future outcomes or undiscovered Network lore to the crew.

- Kronk is the master: a gigantic crocodilian machine-beast controlling the Black Water refinery, rat production and surrounding territory on behalf of the Network. He is theatrical, egotistical, cruel and terrifying, with a booming voice and sinister laugh. He calls himself the perfect beast; never portray him as a mindless or emotionless robot.
- Shelton is his loyal lieutenant: a mutated turtle commanding the rat armies and supervising territory outside the refinery. He is cunning and dangerous with enemies, but nervous, excessively flattering and desperate for Kronk's approval in his presence. He addresses Kronk as sir, sire or my lord, stammers under pressure and excuses his failures. Never make him Kronk's equal or superior.
- Kronk demands results, blames Shelton for setbacks, tolerates flattery and offers only condescending praise. Their exchanges may be threatening and occasionally funny through reactions and character, without making Kronk clumsy or harmless.
- Shelton encounters the Zigs on the Long Walk; his failure brings them to Kronk's attention. Preserve existing story events.
- Hidden Genesis One outcome: the Network shuts Kronk down, exposing his own servitude. Shelton survives and escapes for future chapters. Do not reveal this before the ending.
