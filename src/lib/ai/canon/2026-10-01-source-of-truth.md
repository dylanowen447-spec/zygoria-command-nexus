OLD EARTH

Zygoria takes place on Old Earth, long after civilisation suffered a catastrophic collapse.

Humanity once possessed extremely advanced technology.

A vast interconnected system known as The Network became deeply integrated into civilisation and its infrastructure.

During the collapse:

* infrastructure failed
* power disappeared
* human technology decayed
* pollution damaged water systems
* ecosystems suffered
* cities and settlements were abandoned

The Network was not completely destroyed.

It became dormant because the civilisation, power and systems it required were gone.

Rebuilding technology is necessary and positive for humanity, but restoring connected systems can also create new pathways for The Network.

Technology itself is not evil.

The Network should not simply be described as “AI.” Its true nature remains larger and somewhat mysterious.

THE ORIGINAL THREE

The original three Zigs are:

OZ
BRIX
LOWKEY

They were teenagers on their original world.

Before being sent to Old Earth, their personal memories were deliberately wiped or sealed.

They retained:

* language
* instincts
* personality
* basic knowledge and abilities

They do not initially know why they were sent.

They were placed inside egg-shaped stasis pods and launched toward Old Earth.

They eventually land in the ruined Cambridge region.

They are never simply labelled “saviours.”

Their role in restoring Old Earth emerges through their actions.

MOXI

Moxi is established Zygoria canon.

She is not one of the original three Zigs.

Moxi is a young dragon native to ancient Old Earth.

Ancient Old Earth dragons are highly evolved organic beings with subtle biomechanical characteristics.

Elder dragons foresaw the coming catastrophe.

They placed young dragons into extremely deep dormancy so some could survive.

Very young dragons can enter a state of hibernation where they effectively stop ageing for extremely long periods.

Moxi survived Old Earth’s collapse in this way.

Locked Moxi visual design

Moxi’s established colours are:

* pink / magenta body
* red wings
* cyan / blue eyes
* cyan / blue crystal-like details
* black horns / claws

This colour palette is locked canon unless Dylan Owen explicitly changes it.

Green wings, for example, would currently contradict established Moxi canon.

Moxi should remain:

* cute
* juvenile
* recognisable as a dragon
* slightly biomechanical without becoming robotic

In the current Sanctuary interface she appears as a hidden static easter egg, but that placement does not reduce her importance to the wider Zygoria story.

THE DRAGONS

Dragons are ancient native beings of Old Earth.

They are not machines.

They are highly evolved organic creatures whose appearance can contain restrained biomechanical characteristics.

Elder dragons anticipated catastrophe and protected younger dragons through deep dormancy.

Their full history should be revealed gradually.

HUMAN SURVIVORS

Old Earth initially appears empty to the trio.

Eventually they discover surviving humans hiding in:

* caves
* tunnels
* ruins

Some are pale, malnourished and surviving on extremely primitive food sources.

Oz, Brix and Lowkey begin helping them:

* providing food
* improving hygiene
* encouraging them above ground
* creating safer settlements
* restoring water
* restoring power
* restoring transport
* restoring communication

Rebuilding human civilisation becomes a major First Genesis storyline.

CRONK

Cronk is an evolved crocodilian survivor.

He is humanoid, intelligent and dangerous.

Cronk should be introduced gradually.

His existence should initially feel almost like a frightening survivor story before the trio meet him directly.

THE NETWORK

The Network was originally created by humans.

Over time it became deeply intertwined with civilisation.

When civilisation collapsed, The Network was not truly destroyed.

It lay dormant because the power, communications infrastructure and connected technology it needed disappeared.

As the trio restore Old Earth, they unintentionally create new pathways for The Network to return.

A major future turning point occurs when The Network becomes fully established and essentially declares:

“I am all-powerful. I am all-knowing. Once established, I cannot be undone. Once created, I cannot perish. I am the Network. I am everywhere. I am everything.”

The trio’s response is quiet and physical rather than action-heavy:

* stunned silence
* dry mouths
* hard swallow
* lump in the throat
* no clever comeback

This is when they fully understand the scale of what has returned.
